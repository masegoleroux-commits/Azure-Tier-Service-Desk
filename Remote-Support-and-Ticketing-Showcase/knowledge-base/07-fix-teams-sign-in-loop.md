# Fix Teams sign-in loop

**For:** Anyone stuck at the Teams sign-in screen

1. Quit Teams fully: right-click the Teams icon near the clock > **Quit**.
2. Open **Settings** > **Apps** > **Installed apps** > Microsoft Teams > **Advanced options** > **Reset**.
3. Open **Credential Manager** from the Start menu > Windows Credentials, and remove entries starting with `MicrosoftOffice` or `msteams`.
4. Open Teams and sign in again.
5. Use Teams on the web (https://teams.microsoft.com) while you wait.

**Still not working?** Log a ticket with a screenshot of any error code.
