# Reset your password with self-service

**For:** All staff

1. Go to https://aka.ms/sspr.
2. Enter your work email and the characters shown.
3. Verify with two methods (Authenticator app, text message or phone call).
4. Choose a new password: at least 12 characters, not used before.
5. On a work laptop, lock and unlock it (Windows key + L) and sign in with the new password.

**Still not working?** Log a ticket or call the service desk. Never share your password with anyone, including IT.
