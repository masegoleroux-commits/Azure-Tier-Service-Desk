# How to log a good ticket

**For:** All staff

Help us fix it faster by including:

1. **What's wrong:** what you were doing and what happened.
2. **The exact error message** or a screenshot.
3. **When it started** and whether anything changed (new password, new phone, moved desk).
4. **Who is affected:** just you, or others too.
5. **How urgent:** what you can't do because of it.

Never include passwords or MFA codes in a ticket.
