# Add a printer

**For:** All staff

1. Department printers are added automatically when you sign in.
2. To add one yourself: **Settings** > **Bluetooth & devices** > **Printers & scanners** > **Add device**.
3. Choose the printer named for your floor or department.
4. **Jobs stuck "Printing"?** Open the printer queue, cancel all documents, restart your PC and try again.

**Still not working?** Log a ticket with the printer name and any error.
