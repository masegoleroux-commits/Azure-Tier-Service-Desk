# Moving MFA to a new phone

**For:** Anyone changing phones

**Do this before you hand in or wipe your old phone.**

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator on the new phone.
2. On a computer, go to https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info.
3. **Add sign-in method** > Microsoft Authenticator, and scan the QR code with the new phone.
4. Approve the test notification on the new phone.
5. Delete the old phone from the list.

**Already lost the old phone?** Call the service desk. We'll verify your identity with your manager and reset it.
