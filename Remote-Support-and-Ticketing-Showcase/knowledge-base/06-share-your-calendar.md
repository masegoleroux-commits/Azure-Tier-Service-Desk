# Share your calendar

**For:** All staff

1. In Outlook, go to Calendar.
2. Right-click your calendar > **Sharing permissions**.
3. **Add** the person, then choose their access:
   - **Can view when I'm busy**
   - **Can view all details**
   - **Can edit** (for assistants or managers who book for you)
4. They get an email with a button to add your calendar.

**Need delegate access for a manager?** Log a ticket and IT will set it up.
