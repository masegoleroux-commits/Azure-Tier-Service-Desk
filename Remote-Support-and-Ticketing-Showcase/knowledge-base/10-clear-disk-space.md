# Clear disk space

**For:** Anyone seeing "Low disk space" or a slow PC

1. Empty the Recycle Bin.
2. **Settings** > **System** > **Storage** > **Temporary files** > tick Temporary files and Delivery Optimization > **Remove files**.
3. Move large personal files out of Downloads.
4. Restart your computer at least once a week.

**Still under 10% free?** Log a ticket and IT will clean up remotely.
