# Connect to the VPN

**For:** Staff working away from the office

1. Make sure you're connected to the internet first.
2. Click the network icon near the clock > **VPN** > select **Company VPN** > **Connect**.
3. Approve the sign-in on Microsoft Authenticator.
4. Test by opening a department drive.

**Won't connect?** Restart your PC, try a different network (for example a phone hotspot), then log a ticket with the error.
