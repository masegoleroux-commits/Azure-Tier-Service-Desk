# Map a network drive

**For:** Staff missing a department drive

Department drives (for example F: for Finance) appear automatically when you sign in.

1. Sign out and sign back in.
2. Open File Explorer > **This PC** and look under Network locations.
3. Still missing? Press Windows key + R, type `\\DC01\Finance` (or your department), press Enter.
   - **It opens:** your access is fine; log a ticket so IT can fix the automatic mapping.
   - **Access denied:** you need access approved by your manager. Log a ticket.

**Recently changed department?** Your access may still be updating. Log a ticket.
