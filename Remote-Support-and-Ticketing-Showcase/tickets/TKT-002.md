## Problem
Outlook shows "Your mailbox is full. You may not be able to send or receive items."

## Impact
One user can't send to customers; can still read and reply from her phone until the quota is reached there too.

## Troubleshooting
1. `07-Exchange-Ticket-Fixes.ps1 -Fix MailboxSize`: 49.6 GB of 50 GB used.
2. Archive status: None.
3. Largest folder: Sent Items (18 GB, many attachments).

## Resolution
Enabled the online archive and applied the default 2-year archive policy. Showed the user how to sort by size and remove large attachments. Mailbox now at 31 GB after archiving.

## Customer reply
Hi Megan, I've turned on your Online Archive, so older emails will move there automatically. You can find it in Outlook under "Online Archive". You're now well under the limit and sending works again.
