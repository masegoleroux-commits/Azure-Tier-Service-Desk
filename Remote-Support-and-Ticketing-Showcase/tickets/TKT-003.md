## Problem
New payroll officer can't see the Finance shared mailbox.

## Impact
One user can't process supplier queries sent to the shared address.

## Troubleshooting
1. Confirmed approval from the mailbox owner by email.
2. Checked permissions: user had no FullAccess on finance@.

## Resolution
Granted FullAccess with auto-mapping and SendAs. Mailbox appeared in Outlook after 50 minutes and a restart.

## Customer reply
Hi Zanele, you now have access to the Finance mailbox. It should appear on the left in Outlook after a restart. You can also send as finance@ by choosing it in the From field.
