## Problem
Teams opens, shows the sign-in screen, accepts the password, then returns to sign-in again.

## Impact
One user can't join customer calls in Teams; using Teams on the web as a workaround.

## Troubleshooting
1. Licence check: Teams licence assigned.
2. Sign-in logs: successful sign-ins, no Conditional Access failures.
3. Quick Assist: Credential Manager had several old `MicrosoftOffice16` entries.

## Resolution
Quit Teams, reset the Teams app in Settings > Apps, removed old Office entries in Credential Manager, signed in again successfully.

## Customer reply
Hi Karabo, Teams is working again. Old saved sign-in details were confusing it, so I cleared them and reset the app. If it happens again, try "Fix Teams sign-in loop" in the IT Help Centre.
